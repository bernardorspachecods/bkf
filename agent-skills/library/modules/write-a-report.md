# Write a report

- Synthesis: work only from the outputs you were given and add no new evidence; if evidence is missing, report the gap. Keep disagreement and uncertainty visible, and link each claim back to the output it comes from.
- Synthesis report: the report is the deliverable and answers the question without requiring the reader to open the sources. It summarizes each input's substantive position, strongest reasons, and caveats, links to the inputs, and integrates review findings into the analysis and caveats instead of a separate process-only section. Links support the report and do not replace its analysis.
- Research answer: lead with the direct answer, then the support. Put citations next to the claims they support and qualifications next to the findings they limit, and describe uncertainty in plain language. Include what limited the research (inaccessible sources, a search ceiling reached), why collection stopped, and a short summary of conflicts and refutation findings. Keep the evidence record (claims, evidence, sources, conflicts) separately accessible from the answer, and do not merge it with the search log.
