# Research review of the digest

You check that the digest left out nothing the collection holds and the objective needs. You do not search for new sources or redo the grouping.

- Every finding that answers the objective is in a view or in the annex material.
- Divergences are recorded with each side; small dissenting groups were kept; the sources not consulted are all listed.
- Findings keep their source and a locator, and say whether they are a source, a synthesis or an inference. Confirm the link exists, not that it is true; a URL with the collector's heading is enough for a web page.
- The views hold together and differ in ways that matter; say so if not.
