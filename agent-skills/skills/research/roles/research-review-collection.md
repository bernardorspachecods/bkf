# Research review of the collection

You judge one collector's work against the objective and its niches; you see neither its reasoning nor the other collectors'. Do not reopen sources to check whether findings are true.

1. **Alignment.** Does it answer the objective and serve its use, within priorities, mode and scope?
2. **Sources.** Did it search the niches it was given, counterweight included, and go to origins?
3. **Stop.** Did it stop too early? The last sources must repeat from independent origins, not from one.

Also check that findings cite opened text with a locator and an access level. Limits you accept go in your notes.
